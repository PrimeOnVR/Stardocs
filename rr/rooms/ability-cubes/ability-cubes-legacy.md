# Ability Cubes Legacy

Before the Ability Cubes that went mainstream was created, there was another version that came before it. That version is now known as Ability Cubes Legacy. It was made with a completely different codebase, with different cubes, creators, and ideas.



## History

At the end of Ability Cubes Legacy's lifespan, the RRS version of AC was in development. However, due to controversies between the game's creators, one creator decided to pull all of their work from the new version. However, while the other creators worked around the clock to remove their work upon request, that creator had decided to sabotage AC Legacy by using a glitch to delete the game. From then on, AC Legacy was in limbo for a long time, until later being found on their account under a burner name. Today, we have the original AC Legacy map back, but not any of the images, visits, and community that was once with it.



## Easter Eggs

Ability Cubes Legacy was a game with many easter eggs! Here are some notable ones:



### Lobby Secret

In AC Legacy, there used to be very loose boundaries to where you could be in the map. With those loose boundaries, many tricks were possible, like being able to get on top of the lobby. So, rather than removing the ability to get up there, the devs decided to add an easter egg. That easter egg was a park bench, with a few items on top of it. Those items were:



#### Tom

Tom was an NPC found sitting on the bench on top of the lobby. When interacted with, he would talk to you, saying about how he likes to watch fights from the roof of the lobby, and how you can stay there and watch with him if you would like.



#### Polaroid

On the table of the bench, there was a polaroid. The polaroid was taken by [prime.md](../../../info/people/prime.md "mention") and showed a picture of the bench it was on. It showed 3 people sitting on the bench, which was Tom, [prime.md](../../../info/people/prime.md "mention"), and [ben.md](../../../info/people/ben.md "mention").



#### Water Bottle

On the table of the bench, there was a water bottle. Tom was thirsty, I guess.
