# Usage Info

You can use our wiki in many ways, but here are some things you can and cannot do with our content.



You can:

* Read the wiki to gather information about Starlight and its creations
* Export PDFs of pages of the wiki to have external, offline access of the wiki
* Export the GitHub repo to have external, offline access of the wiki



You cannot:

* Train AI models or let AI models use our resources (wiki, github repo, and other files) without our permission



If these usage guidelines are unclear, or you have a use case that you do not believe is covered, please let us know in our discord server! We are happy to fit your use case, as long as it is in good faith. (and yes, critiquing us and the things we do is allowed!)
