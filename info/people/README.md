# People

There are many individuals involved in the development and betterment of Starlight's various services, and those individuals are listed here!



## How do I get my own page?

You will automatically be made a page when you contribute to Starlight, and that contribution is large enough to be documented.



## How do I get a part of, or my whole page removed?

Let us know in our Discord Server, and we will take care of it for you! We are sad to see your information leave the wiki, however, will happily honor your request.

{% hint style="info" %}
All information is saved in change requests via GitHub, and users of the page can save information to be used later. Nothing on the internet can be truly deleted, and there is nothing we can do about it!
{% endhint %}



## How do I get more information added to my page?

Let us know in our Discord Server, and we will take care of it for you! We are happy to add more information to your page, and recommend that you ask us to add more info, like contact information, additional quotes, and images to your page!
