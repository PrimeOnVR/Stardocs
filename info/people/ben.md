# Ben

Ben was an active player of many early Starlight games, and had contributed to testing several of them in early beta stages. Although considerably less active, he continues to be a part of the Starlight community, helping solve ARGs and much more. He is most notable for his participation in [ability-cubes-legacy.md](../../rr/rooms/ability-cubes/ability-cubes-legacy.md "mention"), and is part of an easter egg hidden inside it.
